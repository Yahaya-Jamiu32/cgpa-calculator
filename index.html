Did you know?

CGPA is weighted by credit units, so a 4-unit course moves your CGPA more than a 1-unit course.

About

GPA = total quality points ÷ total credit units. Quality points = grade point × credit unit.

Cumulative CGPA combines your previous credits and CGPA with the current semester.

Your entries are saved only in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server. Use “Clear all” to erase them.

Grade points differ between universities. Check the scale against your official policy; this app does not assign degree classes.
